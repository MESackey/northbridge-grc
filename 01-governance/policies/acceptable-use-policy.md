## 1. Purpose
Define the rules for using Northbridge equipment, systems, networks, and data safely, ethically, and lawfully.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors temporary staff and third parties operating Northbridge laptops, accounts, network connections, or handling corporate data.

## 3. Policy statements
1. **Business-first equipment use.** Company laptops, phones, and accounts are provided for business purposes. Reasonable, limited personal use is permitted if it does not impact productivity or network security.
2. **Credential secrecy.** Account credentials, passwords, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) tokens must never be shared, written down, or stored in plain text.
3. **Physical screen locking.** Workstations must be locked immediately when leaving a desk or leaving a device unattended (Win+L/Ctrl+Cmd+Q). Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
4. **Immediate incident reporting.** Lost or stollen devices, potential data exposures, and suspicious emails must be reported immediately (within 1 hour) to the Security Team (security@northbridgehealth.co.uk).
5. **Patient data isolation.** Patient data must never be transfered to personal devices, personal email accounts, unapproved cloud storage, or USB removable media.
6. **Software installation controls.** Users must not install unapproved software, browser extensions, or standalone applications on company hardware.
7. **Prohibition of unapproved SaaS.** Only cloud applications reviewed and listed on the Corporate Software Register may be used for company work.
8. **Email and web safety.** Company communications must not be used to send spam, harass others, access illegal or explicit content, or bypass web security filters.
9. **Device handling and travel.** Devices taken outside Northbridge premises must remain under direct supervision or locked in a secure location. Devices must never be left visible in unattended vehicles.

| Role | Responsibility |

## 5. Enforcement
Non-compliance with this policy may result in revoked access privileges, formal disciplinary action, or termination of employment/ contract.
